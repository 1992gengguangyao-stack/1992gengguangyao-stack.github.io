Privacy Policy

We built RizzKey around one rule: your conversations are yours.

🔒 RizzKey collects no data — no analytics, no ads, no tracking
The short version: RizzKey has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your API key, your settings and your messages stay in your browser. When you ask for an AI reply, your request goes directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose — we never see it.

🎯 Overview

This policy explains what information the RizzKey browser extension ("the extension") handles, how it is stored, and what leaves your device (spoiler: only what you send to your own AI provider). It applies to the RizzKey extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers, including its in-page panel, popup and settings page.

🚫 What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or usage statistics — the extension contains no analytics SDKs.
  • No advertising — there are no ads and no ad SDKs.
  • No tracking or profiling — no trackers, no fingerprints, no identifiers.
  • No accounts — the extension never asks you to sign up or log in.
  • No message logs — we have no servers, so there is nothing for your conversations to be sent to.
  • No purchase data — the extension contains no purchases or payment processing.

🌐 Where the extension runs and what it reads

RizzKey's panel is injected as a content script on popular chat sites (WhatsApp Web, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram Web, X, Discord, Tinder and Bumble), so you can generate replies without leaving the chat:

  • When you open the panel, it reads the last visible incoming message on that page — only to pre-fill the reply box. You can always see and edit that text before generating a reply.
  • When you tap Insert, the chosen text is typed into the chat's input box of the page you are on.
  • Nothing is read or sent while the panel is closed. The extension does not monitor keystrokes, and it does not continuously observe page content.
  • Apart from the providers below, the extension requests no other network access.

📱 What is stored on your device

The following is kept in the extension's local storage in your browser, is never uploaded by us, and is removed when you remove the extension:

  • Your AI provider settings: API key, base URL and model name (entered by you in Settings).
  • Preferences: default vibe, optional first names you enter to personalize replies.
  • The last message you pasted into the panel, and your starred phrases.

🤖 How AI requests work

RizzKey works in a bring-your-own-key model. When you generate a reply:

  • The message you copied or pasted, together with your current draft, is sent directly from your browser over HTTPS to the AI provider you configured (for example OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq or OpenRouter).
  • Your API key is sent only to that provider, as required to authenticate the request.
  • We never see, relay, store or process any of this. RizzKey operates no backend servers.
  • When you save your settings, Chrome asks you to grant network access to that provider's domain. You can revoke it at any time from the extension's "Site access" settings.
  • How that provider handles your request is governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

🧒 Children

RizzKey is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

🗑️ Your control

  • Clear your API key or any field at any time in the extension's Settings.
  • Revoke the extension's access to a provider domain at any time via Chrome's extension settings.
  • Remove the extension to delete every piece of locally stored data — instantly and completely.

🔄 Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page and the "effective" date below. Since the extension collects no data, material changes to the policy are unlikely.
